Our Process

Every engagement follows three phases, so you always know what happens next.

1

Feasibility & Planning

Scope review, feasibility assessment, risk identification, and the baseline schedule and budget.

2

Execution & Supervision

Progress tracking against the plan, quality audits, vendor and contractor coordination, and corrective action.

3

Commissioning & Handover

Testing and commissioning checks, final documentation, and a formal handover to the client.

What You Receive

Clear, documented outputs at each stage of the project.

Risk Assessment Matrix

Identified risks, their likelihood and impact, and the mitigation owner for each.

Critical Path Schedule

A detailed schedule showing the critical path, with regular progress updates.

Quality Control Audits

Audit reports with non-conformities, corrective actions and closure status.

Budget & Cost Reports

Planned versus actual cost tracking, with variance analysis.

Progress Dashboards

Periodic management reports on output, delays and work in progress.

Handover Documentation

Commissioning records, as-built documents and the closing project report.
