About RST

Engineering and project management for EPC projects.

Who We Are

RST is a project management consultancy focused on EPC (Engineering, Procurement and Construction) projects. Our work is concentrated in manufacturing, with a secondary focus on civil infrastructure.

We bring together planning discipline, technical understanding and clear reporting, so that clients always know where a project stands.

How We Work

Engineering Focus

Design coordination and technical review across EPC scopes.

Planning & Control

Schedules, budgets and progress tracking with corrective action when needed.

Team Collaboration

Working alongside client, contractor and vendor teams throughout the project.

  • Experience in complex EPC projects across manufacturing and civil sectors
  • Focus on on-time delivery without compromising quality
  • Practical cost optimization and risk management
  • Rigorous quality standards throughout the project lifecycle
